What L2 models

L2 keeps the L1 roofline ceilings but decomposes one transformer layer into its kernels (QKV projection, attention scores, PV, output projection, MLP — dense or routed MoE, plus the vocab logits) and gives each kernel three resource clocks:

t_kernel = max(FLOPs_G / (P · occupancy),
                DRAM_G / b_eff,
                SMEM_G / SMEM_bandwidth)

occupancy is the fraction of the target CTAs/SM that fit given per-SM shared memory (tile size), registers (regs/thread), threads/block, and TMEM (Blackwell accumulators, used for FP8/INT4). DRAM traffic is reduced by an L2 reuse model: hit = min(1, L2_usable / bytes_touched_per_layer). FlashAttention removes the S×S score matrices from HBM and instead pays SMEM tile traffic (with a TMEM discount on Blackwell). Activations can be fused on-chip per layer and checkpointed (recompute) for the memory budget. MoE pays the router plus top-k experts' FLOPs and weights.

Layer time is the serial sum of kernel times; total time is L · layer_time + logits. Kernel times are deliberately conservative (no inter-kernel overlap) and all microarchitectural numbers are first-pass estimates to be refined. Full equations: docs/simulation-methodology.md
